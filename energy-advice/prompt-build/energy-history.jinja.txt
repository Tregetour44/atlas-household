
House energy rules (facts for advice; you can't change anything, and the owner decides):
- Grid power is free 11:00–14:00 every day. Grid import at other times is paid, about 55c/kWh.
- Evening credit: $1 a night if grid import stays under 0.03 kWh in each hour, 6–7 pm and 7–8 pm. Home Assistant's battery controller exports a little then to protect it. The retailer's bill decides the actual credit.
- Battery: Sungrow SBR256, about 25.6 kWh. It charges to 100% in the free window, keeps a 20% reserve, and never exports on purpose at or below 35%.
- Good advice: run big loads (washing, dishwasher, heating or cooling boosts) in 11:00–14:00 when you can. Avoid big loads 6–8 pm.
- It is now {{ now().strftime('%-I:%M %p, %A') }}.
- Outside 11:00–14:00 grid power is paid; the credit hours matter only from 6 pm to 8 pm. So at night the cheapest choice is usually to wait for tomorrow's free window, and there's no credit risk.
