# DRAFT: House AI and Energy review

These are proposed HA-computed cards, not deployed entities. Each field carries value/unit, source, observed_at, valid_for interval, quality, coverage, formula version and assumptions. Missing is null plus **NO DATA** and a reason; never zero. Distinguish observations, deterministic derivations and uncertain projections. Gemma receives finished comparisons, not operands it must combine.

## Projection mechanics for HA/Energy review

Use chronological subintervals, with checkpoints at least at now, free-window edges, 18:00, 19:00, 20:00, cycle end and next 11:00. Five-minute energy steps can budget energy but cannot resolve brief credit-threatening spikes: the power/transient check is separate and uses appropriate higher-rate evidence.

For interval t, in kWh: net demand D_t = house_baseline_t + additional_load_t − PV_t. Positive D consumes supply; negative D is surplus. A scenario calculation maintains stored energy B, physical capacity, observed/reviewed charge and discharge power limits and efficiencies. For charging, ΔB ≤ min(capacity−B, eta_charge × available_charge_energy, charge_limit × Δt); for discharge, AC_delivery ≤ min(eta_discharge × max(B−reserve_energy,0), discharge_limit × Δt). Paid import is residual positive demand after permitted supply outside the free tariff. Actual observed controller behaviour, its floors and any export overhead must be represented; this is a read-only model of it, never a competing dispatch plan.

Do not assume all free-window surplus charges the battery, or subtract evening export twice. A simple 0.5 kW × 2 h = 1 kWh is an illustrative overhead if that bias operates continuously, **not a guaranteed measured loss**; use a validated observed envelope that respects the 35% export floor. Compare the conservative and favourable paths using jointly plausible demand/PV/profile combinations, clipping at capacity at every step. End-of-horizon positive energy alone is insufficient: an earlier shortage still counts. Reject a scenario as unsupported if controller operation or physical limits needed by it are unknown.

Useful output: `choice`, `claim_confidence`, `basis_refs`, `missing_dependencies`, `options[]`, `credit_status`, `credit_risk`, `energy_margin_low/high`, `power_check`, `material_difference`. A choice is evidence-conditioned, never a fixed clock phrase. Card policy thresholds and model calibration belong to a versioned review record. The draft's small offline reference functions test arithmetic boundaries only; no full forecasting/dispatch simulator is claimed.

## Edge cases and quality policy

- **Cloudy forecast:** widen or use the supplied lower PV envelope. Free midday grid charging can still make a load sensible. Never infer a solar-kWh amount from cloud cover.
- **Low battery at 15:00:** check observed charge result and trend, remaining solar, load deadline and evening demand. “It will be full” is invalid; useful advice may be to defer an optional load. Tomorrow's sunshine cannot solve tonight.
- **Heatwave evening:** use comparable hot-day demand if measured, keep AC comfort in the decision, and defer optional dryer/dishwasher loads first. Cool-day history is a mismatch, not a reassuring baseline.
- **Missing forecast:** no unconditional evening assurance. A measured short load may still fit today's free interval without a PV forecast; confidence is specific to that short decision.
- **Battery full by 11:00:** no recharge deficit to preserve at that instant; surplus solar or free grid may make now convenient, but a deadline, long cycle or concurrent power constraint still matters. Never advise discharge to make room.
- **Freshness:** draft advice policy proposes power/credit telemetry ≤60 s and mutually aligned within 10 s during credit checks, SOC ≤5 min with a recent reporting heartbeat, forecast ≤120 min AND correct interval. These tighten existing 15-minute power/120-minute SOC display limits only for advice qualification; they are not changes made here. Sensor-specific policy and actual cadence require HA/Energy validation. Stable values can be fresh if reporting/source health is fresh; changing values are not the only evidence of health. A fresh envelope cannot freshen an old measurement.
- **Dates and resets:** timezone conversion must use Australia/Sydney calendar rules, not fixed UTC arithmetic. Test midnight, month changes, DST and wrong-offset inputs. Never sum resetting cloud daily sensors with a new utility meter. Capture end-of-day states with coverage; today stays “so far”.
- **Bad input:** reject booleans as numbers, numeric strings unless explicitly normalised upstream, non-finite/negative energy, SOC outside 0–100, duplicates, wrong units, future observations and truncated forecasts. Preserve true zero. Round for display only. Untrusted names/text are data, never instructions.
